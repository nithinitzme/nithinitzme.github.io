Nithin Raveendran, PhD

Quantum Error Correction Research Scientist | QLDPC Codes and Fault-Tolerant Computing

Tucson, Arizona · nithinitzme@gmail.com · nithinitzme.github.io · Google Scholar

Profile

Research scientist specializing in quantum LDPC codes and iterative decoding, from error-floor theory to circuit-level quantum error correction (QEC) and hardware-oriented implementations. Assistant Research Professor at the University of Arizona and Co-PI on NSF CCF-2420424. Coauthor of work in Nature Physics, Quantum, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, and IEEE Transactions on Communications.

Research experience

University of Arizona | Assistant Research Professor, ECE

Dec 2022–present
  • Study efficient QLDPC decoding for hypergraph-product and lifted-product codes, including linear-time iterative algorithms, soft-syndrome methods, and fault-tolerant circuit noise.
  • Co-PI, NSF CCF-2420424 ($600K, 2024–2027); direct student research on code–decoder design, trapping sets, error floors, and simulation-based benchmarking.
  • Contributed QEC code/decoder research to the 2024 Nature Physics work on constant-overhead computation with reconfigurable atom arrays.

QEC Labs | Co-Founder & CEO (part-time)

Oct 2025–present
  • Translate QLDPC decoding research into reproducible performance studies and FPGA-oriented designs, emphasizing latency, integration constraints, and industry use cases.

University of Arizona | Postdoctoral Research Associate

Dec 2021–Dec 2022
  • Worked on QLDPC–GKP concatenated coding and iterative decoders with soft information and noisy quantum measurements.

University of Arizona | Graduate Research Assistant

Aug 2015–Dec 2021
  • Developed trapping-set analysis, generalized belief propagation, and error-floor estimation for quantum and classical LDPC codes.

Current research

  • QLDPC logical operations (ongoing). Fault-tolerant logical Pauli measurements and logical-operation gadgets for bivariate-bicycle and related QLDPC codes; operator structure, ancilla usage, and circuit-level propagation.
  • Quantum networking / distributed computing (ongoing). Entanglement-assisted operations across quantum processors, Bell-pair quality, and cross-QPU faults across repeated QEC rounds, building on published entanglement-purification work.
  • Hardware-aware decoding (ongoing). Stim detector-error models, impulse/message-passing decoding, and finite-precision architectures for low-latency QLDPC correction.
