A short introduction for hosts

Nithin Raveendran, Ph.D., is an Assistant Research Professor at the University of Arizona and co-founder and CEO of QEC Labs. His research focuses on quantum LDPC codes, iterative decoding, and hardware-aware quantum error correction. His work has appeared in Nature Physics, Quantum, and IEEE journals. He brings a researcher’s and entrepreneur’s perspective to practical fault-tolerant quantum computing.

November 2026 · Research note

A good code is the beginning of the conversation

A quantum error-correcting code tells us what is possible. A decoder determines how much of that possibility we can use. Between the two sit practical questions: how reliable are the measurements, how much time is available, and which errors are hardest to correct?

That is why error floors and trapping sets matter. They give us a way to examine failures that a single headline performance number can hide. Understanding those failures can help guide both decoder design and the choice of code.

The engineering conversation begins here: with a code, a noise model, a decoder, and a careful account of where the system still breaks down.
